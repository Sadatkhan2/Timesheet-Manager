Timesheet Merger

Reads the monthly attendance sheet (days 1 to 31 with hours, A and OFF) and fills Absent, Over Time, Work Days and Remarks in the target timesheet, matching by Iqama number. The target keeps its formatting.

1. Source sheet

Attendance sheet with the dates across the top.

2. Target sheet

Timesheet to be filled.

Everything runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded. The first worksheet of each file is used. Days with a number above the normal hours add the extra hours to overtime. Employees with no absence or overtime are left untouched. People found only in the source are added to a new sheet in the downloaded file.
